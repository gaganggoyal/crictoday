# Inviting academies, clubs and grounds

Emails and messages to invite the first profiles to cricketmatch.today. Fill in every `[bracket]` before sending.

## Before you send

- **Send each one yourself, one at a time,** from a real mailbox. Do not use a bulk mailing. Personal emails get replies; bulk ones land in spam, and can pull cricketmatch.today's sign-in emails into spam with them.
- **Make the first line about them:** a recent win, a camp they posted on Instagram, the ground they use. That line decides whether the rest gets read.
- **Use the address they publish** on their website, Instagram bio or Google Maps listing. State associations also list their affiliated academies.
- **Send Tuesday to Thursday, mid-morning.** Coaches are at the nets early in the morning and in the evening.
- **Follow up once,** four or five days later, then stop. Keep a sheet of who you wrote to and when.
- **Approve new profiles the same day** at [/admin/profiles](https://cricketmatch.today/admin/profiles). A quick "you're live" email keeps them keen.

## Subject lines

Pick one, or try two against each other:

- A free page for [Academy name] on cricketmatch.today
- Parents in [City] are looking for coaching like yours
- [Academy name]'s camps and trials, in front of local cricket fans
- Can we list [Academy name]? It's free and takes 10 minutes
- [Tournament name]: every fixture on one page, free
